How the morning runs

  • The trial first — weeks ahead, at your home: photos, references, timings and the exact look agreed
  • A written schedule — who’s in the chair when, so the photographer gets you finished, not flustered
  • We come fully equipped — professional tools, products and backup kit at your venue or home
  • Calm by design — one stylist per timeline, a second added for larger parties
