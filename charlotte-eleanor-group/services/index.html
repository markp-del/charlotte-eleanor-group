The small print, up front

How pricing works with us.

Every colour service starts with a consultation — at booking and again at the door — so the price you agree is the price you pay. Skin sensitivity tests are required 48 hours ahead of first-time colour, exactly as a salon would. A colour correction is never quoted online: Charlotte sees the hair and tests a strand first.

  • Colour prices are for the colour alone — the wash and blow-dry is a separate service
  • A small booking deposit secures your slot, deducted from the bill
  • Card payment at the door; SMS confirmations and reminders as standard
  • Group and household bookings welcome — one visit, several chairs
