How it works

Book. Open the door.
Sit back.

1 · Book online

Pick your service and time, secure it with a small deposit, and get SMS confirmation and reminders. No phone tag, no waiting on hold.

2 · We arrive equipped

Your stylist arrives in a fully-kitted electric van — professional tools, portable wash station, salon products — and sets up wherever suits you.

3 · Salon finish, home comfort

The same consultation, craft and finish you’d get in the chair — then we pack away, tap the card machine and rebook you before we leave.

Why clients switch

Professional.
Punctual. Personal.

This isn’t a hobbyist with a kit bag. Every Charlotte Eleanor stylist is NVQ-qualified through our own academy, insured for chemical work, and backed by the scheduling technology of a modern salon group.

Perfect for busy families, working-from-home diaries, less-mobile clients and anyone who’d rather spend the school run somewhere other than a salon waiting area.

  • Academy-trained — every stylist qualified through Clwydian Academy, our own school
  • Reliable by design — deposits and reminders mean your slot is really yours
  • Transparent pricing — a published guide, confirmed before we book you in
  • Clean and green — electric vans, tanked greywater, salon hygiene standards at home
