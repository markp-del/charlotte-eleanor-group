How it works

Book. Open the door.
Sit back.

1 · Book online

Pick your service and time, secure it with a small deposit, and get SMS confirmation and reminders. No phone tag, no waiting on hold.

2 · We arrive equipped

Your stylist arrives in a fully-kitted electric van — professional tools, portable wash station, salon products — and sets up wherever suits you.

3 · Salon finish, home comfort

The same consultation, craft and finish you’d get in the chair — then we pack away, tap the card machine and rebook you before we leave.
