Why we teach

The pipeline
is the point.

Apprenticeship starts across UK hairdressing have collapsed by nearly 70% in a decade. Rather than compete for a shrinking pool, the group manufactures its own talent — and every learner leaves with more than a certificate: a mapped route into employment, a chair, or a Charlotte Eleanor franchise of their own.

  • Clwydian Academy CBC — an asset-locked community benefit company in St Asaph
  • Led by a practising Senior Lecturer and Internal Quality Assurer
  • Fully funded routes for eligible learners and apprentices
  • Graduate pathways straight onto the franchisee ladder
