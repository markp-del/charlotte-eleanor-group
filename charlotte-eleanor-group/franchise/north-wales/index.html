Why these four counties

Close enough to support.
Big enough to scale.

Every launch territory sits within easy reach of home base in St Asaph and the academy that trains every stylist we deploy. That’s deliberate: first-cohort franchisees get hands-on launch support, cover from the network when life happens, and a supply chain that reaches them the same day.

Together the four counties hold roughly half a million people — and a high street that’s been shedding salons for years. A stabilised diary needs only 100–120 recurring clients within a thirty-minute drive. Each county holds that many times over.

  • £14,500 all-in — NVQ training, corporate-leased electric van in full wrap, elite kit, starting stock, technology and territory
  • 8% royalty + 2% marketing levy — a 10% total deduction, taken automatically at the point of sale
  • Defended territories — allocated once; the ladder to multi-van operation runs inside your own patch
  • Trained up the road — Clwydian Academy, our own school in St Asaph
