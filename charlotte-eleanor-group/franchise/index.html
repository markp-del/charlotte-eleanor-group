Questions, answered straight

What stylists ask us first.

How much does it cost?

£14,500 all-in: the full fast-track NVQ at Clwydian Academy (£4,500 — reduced if you’re already qualified) plus the £10,000 territory licence that funds your own deployment — wrapped electric van, racking, elite kit, starting stock and the configured technology stack.

What are the ongoing fees?

8% royalty + 2% marketing levy — a 10% total deduction taken automatically at the point of sale. No invoices, no arrears. Stock replenishes at corporate wholesale rates.

Do I need to be qualified?

No. Qualified stylists skip to deployment with entry reduced accordingly; career changers train first at Clwydian Academy in St Asaph, then step into the deployed business.

Where are the territories?

Flintshire, Denbighshire, Conwy and Wrexham — four defended counties around our St Asaph base. See the territories. Each is allocated once.

Owner-operator or management?

You start owner-operator — one van, one territory. The ladder then scales you: an apprentice trains at the academy and deploys in a second van in your patch; established operators run multi-van teams.

Is the income guaranteed?

No — and be wary of any franchise that says otherwise. Our figures are illustrative projections with the assumptions shown; we share the full workings at discovery, before you commit anything. Weighing it against renting a chair or going alone? Read the honest comparison.
