Towns we cover in Flintshire

Flintshire is the busiest corner of North Wales — 155,000 people threaded along the Dee from the border to the coast — and it’s made for mobile. We come to Mold, Buckley, Flint, Connah’s Quay, Shotton, Queensferry, Deeside, Hawarden, Ewloe, Broughton, Saltney, Northop, Holywell, Caerwys and the villages between — with the Chester fringe just over the bridge.

If your week runs on the A55 and A494 — school runs, shifts on Deeside Industrial Park, working from home between meetings — the last thing it needs is a salon waiting room. Book online, and the salon is in your kitchen by the time the kettle’s boiled.

Every service, at home

  • Cuts & styling — ladies’ cut & blow-dry from £35, gents from £15, children from £12
  • Colour & technical — root retouches from £38, foils, balayage from £75, modern perms
  • Occasions — upstyling, proms and wedding mornings, including bridal packages at venues like Soughton Hall or at your own mirror
  • Whole-household visits — one booking, everyone done, one card payment

Good to know

What a home appointment
looks like in Flintshire.

Your stylist arrives in a fully-kitted electric van — professional tools, portable wash station with its own tanks, salon products — and sets up wherever suits you. The same consultation, craft and finish you’d get in the chair, then we pack away, tap the card machine and rebook you before we leave.

  • All we need — a chair near a plug socket and ordinary tap water
  • Deposits & reminders — a small card deposit secures your slot; SMS confirmations as standard
  • First-time colour — skin test 48 hours ahead, arranged at booking
  • Households welcome — one visit, several chairs; card payment at the door
