Towns we cover in Conwy county

From the pier to the valley: we come to Llandudno, Colwyn Bay, Rhos-on-Sea, Penrhyn Bay, Deganwy, Conwy, Llandudno Junction, Abergele, Towyn, Kinmel Bay, Llanrwst and the villages along the A470. The coast has the highest concentration of people in Wales who’d rather not drive to a salon — and now they don’t have to.

Conwy is the oldest county in the region — nearly three in ten residents are over 65 — and it’s also its most premium coast. Both of those facts point the same way: hairdressing that arrives at the door, on time, with salon standards intact. That includes residential homes and sheltered living, where a familiar face every six weeks matters as much as the finish.

Good to know

What a home appointment
looks like in Conwy.

Your stylist arrives in a fully-kitted electric van — professional tools, portable wash station with its own tanks, salon products — and sets up wherever suits you. The same consultation, craft and finish you’d get in the chair, then we pack away, tap the card machine and rebook you before we leave.

  • All we need — a chair near a plug socket and ordinary tap water
  • Deposits & reminders — a small card deposit secures your slot; SMS confirmations as standard
  • First-time colour — skin test 48 hours ahead, arranged at booking
  • Households welcome — one visit, several chairs; card payment at the door
