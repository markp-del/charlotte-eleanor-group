Areas we cover · Mold & the villages

Salon craft,
village by village.

Charlotte Eleanor comes to you in Mold and the thirty-six villages around it — from Cilcain and Caerwys in the west, up onto Halkyn Mountain, east to Buckley and Drury, and over the tops to Llanarmon-yn-Iâl. There is no travel charge anywhere on that round.

The round

Thirty-six villages.
One doorstep: yours.

Most of these places have no salon of their own, and the ones that do have one are a drive away up a single-track lane. That is exactly the shape of place a mobile service is built for.

West

The Alyn valley

Gwernaffield, Pantymwyn, Cilcain, Rhydymwyn, Nannerch, Caerwys, Afonwen

Lanes climbing towards Moel Famau and the Clwydian Range.

North-west

Halkyn Mountain

Rhosesmor, Rhes-y-cae, Wern y Gaer, Halkyn, Pentre Halkyn, Brynford

Scattered limestone villages on the mountain road, a long way from any salon chair.

North & east

Northop & Sychdyn

Sychdyn, Northop, Northop Hall, Mynydd Isa, New Brighton, Bryn-y-Baal, Alltami

The busiest corner of the round, and the one where staying home saves a run onto the A55.

East

Buckley & Drury

Buckley, Drury, Padeswood

The largest settlements on the round, two miles from Mold.

South

Nercwys to Leeswood

Gwernymynydd, Nercwys, Treuddyn, Leeswood, Coed-talon, Pontblyddyn

Small villages, long-standing clients, and word that travels fast.

Over the tops

Llanferres & the Alun

Llanferres, Loggerheads, Cadole, Maeshafn, Eryrys, Llanarmon-yn-Iâl

Ten minutes out on the A494 and routinely missed by anyone working to a council boundary.

How coverage works

One patch, worked
properly.

The van is in a different corner of the round each working day rather than criss-crossing the whole of it. That is the reason a village booking actually holds: your slot is offered on the day the van is already in your valley, not squeezed into a gap between two runs across the tops.

We would rather be the hairdresser every household on one round uses than a name that turns up once, three valleys away. So this is the whole list — if your village is not on it, it is not somewhere we are pretending to cover.

  • Every service travels — cut and blow-dry from £35, gents from £18, root retouch from £38, foils, balayage, perms and bridal
  • No travel charge — anywhere on the round, however far up the lane you are
  • Colour prices — for the colour alone; the wash and blow-dry is a separate service
  • All we need — a chair near a plug socket and ordinary tap water; the van carries everything else
  • First-time colour — a skin test 48 hours ahead, arranged when you book
  • Just outside? Ask anyway — if we are already in your lane that week we can usually fit you in
