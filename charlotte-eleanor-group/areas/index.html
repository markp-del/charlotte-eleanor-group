How coverage works

One base. Four counties.
Routes that make sense.

We schedule by route, not by whoever shouts loudest — appointments in the same area are clustered so your stylist arrives on time with range to spare in the van’s battery. That’s why booking online with your postcode is the fastest way to a confirmed slot.

  • Every service travels — cuts from £15, ladies’ cut & blow-dry from £35, colour, foils, balayage, perms and bridal
  • All we need — a chair near a plug socket and ordinary tap water; the van carries everything else
  • First-time colour — a skin test 48 hours ahead, arranged when you book
  • The Chester fringe too — the border doesn’t stop the van
